Matching-filter relations in the form  Gin(B,Bt,kin) = Gout(A,At,kout)
generated from the four bit-wise equations of Appendix (eq:BL, eq:BR, eq:DeltBL, eq:DeltBR).

Conventions
  +   XOR,  *  AND,  all state/key indices are taken mod n.
  AL[j], AR[j]  : left / right half of A = X^(rA);  Bt/At denote the tilde states.
  dAL[j] = AL[j]+ALt[j]  (and likewise dAR, dBL, dBR) are differences.
  kin[r,j] / kout[r,j] : the round-key bit k^(r)_j, owned by the in-side / out-side.
  L1..L4 below = the paper's L_0..L_3 (same order).
